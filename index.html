About
WEA
Your Screen. Your Earnings.
WEA is a movie advertising company connecting global movie studios and sponsors with real, engaged viewers — rewarding you for the time and attention you already give to entertainment.
🌍 Our Story
WEA began its journey in the Philippines, growing steadily across multiple countries by staying true to one simple belief: everyone deserves the chance to earn, grow, and build a better future — no matter where they start. Today, we're expanding into Africa, beginning with Uganda and Kenya.
🎯 Our Growth Goal
We're working toward opening official WEA offices right here in Uganda and Kenya. Our goal is to reach 12 million people across Uganda — every person who joins, watches, earns, and shares WEA brings us one step closer.
What is WEA?
WEA (Watch Movies, Earn Money) is a platform built around a simple idea: the time you already spend watching videos can earn you real money. Instead of scrolling passively, WEA turns your attention into an income stream — one video at a time. Every video you watch contributes toward your daily earnings, which accumulate in your account and can be withdrawn to mobile money whenever you're ready.
How It Works
Every new account begins as a Trainee, with a modest daily video allowance and starter-level rewards — enough to get a feel for the platform without any upfront cost. From there, you can choose to invest in higher levels, each one unlocking a larger daily video quota and a higher payout per video watched. The further you progress through the levels, the greater your daily, monthly, and annual earning potential becomes.
Beyond watching videos, WEA also rewards you for growing the community. Every user gets a personal referral link and QR code (once they've purchased their first level) to share with friends and family. When someone in your referral network — up to three levels deep — purchases a level of their own, you earn an instant bonus credited straight to your account.
For added convenience, WEA also offers Auto Watch Movies — an optional feature on the Watch & Earn page that instantly completes your remaining daily video quota without manual watching. A 10% service fee applies per video, and the reward is credited to your Retained Wallet right away.
Watch every day without missing one, and every 5th consecutive day earns you an extra streak bonus on top of your normal rewards. You can also lock funds from your General Account or Retained Wallet into DATIM movie company stake plans for additional returns, and earn Lucky Spin chances — for a shot at cash or exclusive prizes — whenever your direct referrals upgrade their level.
📈 About Our Levels
You'll notice our paid levels are labeled VM1 through VM12 — VM stands for Verified Member. Each level represents a step up in daily video quota and earning potential, and every level you purchase verifies and upgrades your standing on the platform. Whichever level you choose, your rewards scale with it, from your first upgrade all the way to VM12.
Our Mission
WEA is a movies advertising company that partners with movie studios and sponsors around the world to promote content to real, engaged viewers. Rather than relying on passive ad impressions, we connect entertainment brands with an audience that actively chooses to watch — and we make sure that audience is compensated fairly for their time and attention. Every video watched on WEA supports a real promotional campaign, creating value for studios, sponsors, and our users all at once.
Terms & Rules Summary
  • Daily video limits are determined by your current level; once you reach your limit, you'll need to wait for the next daily reset to continue earning
  • Deposits are added to your General Account balance and used to purchase or upgrade levels; your Retained Wallet balance holds earnings that are available for withdrawal
  • Withdrawal requests are reviewed and processed within 48 hours. If a withdrawal takes longer than that, please contact support immediately
  • Referral bonuses are paid instantly, across up to three levels of your referral network, at rates set out on the Wages page — these rates may be adjusted from time to time
  • Trainee accounts cannot refer others; purchasing your first level unlocks your referral link and QR code
  • Accounts found to be in violation of platform rules — including fraudulent activity or abuse of the referral system — may be locked pending review
  • WEA reserves the right to update these terms, level structures, and reward rates as the platform evolves
💼 Positions & Salary
Once you purchase your first VM level, you become a Permanent Employee and unlock the Salary page. Grow your referral network and you can qualify for a Position — Team Leader, Assistant Manager, Manager, Senior Manager, Regional Director, Vice President, or Executive Director — each with its own referral requirement and fixed monthly salary. Positions are detected and activated automatically whenever you qualify, no need to claim anything. Once qualified, your salary is credited to your General Account on or after the 6th of every month, for as long as you keep meeting the requirement.

FAQ
Getting Started
What is WEA?›
WEA (Watch Movies, Earn Money) is a platform that pays you for watching movies, trailers, and clips. Every video you watch earns you a small reward, and you can grow your earnings by purchasing higher levels.
How do I start earning?›
Just register with your phone number, watch your first video, and your reward is added automatically. No deposit is required to begin as a Trainee.
Account & Levels
What is a Trainee account?›
Every new user starts as a Trainee — a free 3-day trial period with a small daily video limit and modest rewards. Once the 3 days end, you'll need to purchase a level to keep earning.
How do I upgrade my level?›
Go to the Stages page and purchase any level using your General Account balance. Higher levels unlock more videos per day and a higher payout per video.
What happens to my General Account when I upgrade again?›
When you upgrade from one level to a higher one, the cost of your previous level is automatically refunded back to your Retained Wallet.
Videos & Earnings
How many videos can I watch per day?›
This depends on your current level — shown on the Stages page next to each level.
When does my daily video count reset?›
Every day at 00:00 East Africa Time, based on our server clock — not your phone's clock.
Where do my earnings go?›
Video rewards go to your Retained Wallet, which is withdrawable. Deposits go to your General Account, used to purchase levels.
What is Auto Watch Movies?›
Auto Watch Movies lets you instantly complete your remaining daily video quota without manually watching each one. Tap it on the Watch & Earn page, confirm, and your reward is credited straight to your Retained Wallet right away.
Does Auto Watch Movies have a fee?›
Yes — a 10% service fee is deducted from each video's reward when you use Auto Watch. Your referral commissions to your upline still apply as normal.
Deposits & Withdrawals
What deposit methods are available?›
Easy Pay (MTN/Airtel Mobile Money) is fully automated. Superior Recharge and Bank transfer are available as alternative methods.
How long do withdrawals take?›
Withdrawal requests are reviewed and processed within 48 hours. If yours takes longer, please contact support.
Is there a withdrawal fee?›
Yes — a service charge applies to every withdrawal, shown clearly before you confirm.
Referral Program
How do referral bonuses work?›
You earn a percentage of what your referrals spend when they purchase a level — across up to 3 levels of your network.
Why did I earn less than expected on a referral's purchase?›
Your bonus is capped by your own current level. If your referral outspends your own level, the difference becomes a "Missed Commission" — recoverable if you upgrade within 8 days.
Can Trainee accounts refer others?›
No — you need to purchase at least one level before your referral link becomes active.
