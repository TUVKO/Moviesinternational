About ABB WORKING ROBOTICS

ABB WORKING ROBOTICS is a digital investment platform where you hire real robots to work on your behalf. We're partnered with Boston Dynamics — the machines you hire are powered by their real-world robotics technology, built for precision and reliability. Wealth-building shouldn't be complicated: put a robot to work, let it run its course, and watch steady, reliable labor build your returns over time.

Where We're Based

ABB WORKING ROBOTICS is headquartered in Toronto, Canada. We're proud to be expanding internationally, with new offices opening soon in Uganda to better serve our growing community there.

Join Our Team in Uganda

As we open our Uganda offices, we'll be hiring local representatives to run day-to-day operations, support our members, and represent ABB WORKING ROBOTICS on the ground. If you're interested in being part of our growth in Uganda, stay tuned for opportunities.

How It Works

You hire a machine that fits your budget and it starts working immediately — no waiting, no lock-up. Built on real robotics technology from Boston Dynamics and Inspire Robots, and tagged with a verified serial from ID Plate. It pays a fixed daily return straight into your Scrayd Wallet every day it's on the job, and once its work period is complete, your original purchase amount is paid back into your Prime Account, ready to withdraw or reinvest.

HOW IT WORKS
There's a quiet kind of confidence that comes from watching something work hard on your behalf — showing up, doing its job, and never once making you wait to see the reward. That's the promise behind every machine on AWR.

When you hire a robot, it doesn't sit idle behind a countdown or disappear into a locked vault until some distant maturity date. It clocks in the very same day you bring it on board, and it starts earning for you immediately — a steady, dependable daily return, paid straight into your Scrayd Wallet, day after day, for as long as its contract runs.

We build our fleet on real robotics engineering — our humanoid workers run on technology from Boston Dynamics, the world's leading name in advanced robotics, while precision components like dexterous grasping hands and actuators come from Inspire Robots, trusted by robotics manufacturers worldwide. Every machine you hire also carries a verified, tamper-proof serial tag produced by ID Plate, so you always know your robot is genuine and accounted for.

And when your robot's contract reaches its final day, there's no fine print waiting to surprise you — your full purchase amount is returned straight back to your Prime Account, ready for you to withdraw or put straight back to work on your next hire.

No locking. No waiting. Just a machine, working hard, every single day — for you.
ADDING FUNDS
We give you three simple ways to add funds to your Prime Account. WTRAS works by pasting the confirmation SMS you receive immediately after sending your mobile money payment — our system reads it automatically. GITRAX is for anyone who'd rather upload a screenshot of that same confirmation message instead. SUPA PAY is the simplest of all — just confirm the phone number you paid from. Whichever method you choose, always double-check that you're sending money only to the number displayed inside the app.
EARN WITH REFERRALS
You don't need to buy a machine yourself to start earning — simply share your personal invite link. Every time someone in your network buys a machine, you earn a percentage automatically, credited straight to your Prime Account. Our referral system runs three levels deep: 12% on direct referrals (Level A), 4% on their referrals (Level B), and 2% on the next level after that (Level C) — no cap, and no purchase required from you to start referring.
STAY SAFE
Never share your password, PIN, or one-time verification code (OTP) with anyone for any reason — not with friends, not with someone claiming to be a relative, and not even with someone claiming to represent our support team. Our real support staff will never contact you privately asking for these details. If anyone approaches you claiming to be from ABB WORKING ROBOTICS and asks for your password or OTP, treat it as a scam attempt and report it to our official support channels immediately.
